1 · Data streams

Static: EHR / discharge recordAge, sex, ejection fraction, eGFR, NT-proBNP, sodium, diabetes, hypertension, furosemide dose, prior HF admissions, polygenic risk percentile (genetic marker). Synthetic, generated in twin.py.
Dynamic: smartwatch (nightly)Night heart rate, HRV (RMSSD), breathing rate, SpO2, sleep efficiency, steps. 10% missing days.
Dynamic: smart scale (daily)Morning weight, with scale/clothing offsets and 25% skipped days.
Roadmap sourcesSynthea FHIR bundles, MIMIC-IV, ABHA-linked records via ABDM; Apple Health / Google Fit / Health Connect; 3-tap WhatsApp symptom check.

2 · Ingest

Clean & alignOne row per patient-day; missing values kept as gaps, never imputed.
EHR → twin priorsEF sets heart-rate response to fluid; EHR risk score sets how quickly the twin believes a rising trend.
LLM extractionFree-text discharge summary → FHIR (Patient, Condition, Observation, MedicationStatement).
Consent & privacyABDM consent manager; DPDP Act-compliant storage; de-identified analytics.

3 · Patient digital twin (per patient)

1
Sync · days 0-2 at home learn the person's own baseline for every sensor.
2
State estimation (Kalman filter) · hidden state = [excess fluid L, trend L/day] with uncertainty. Every sensor is a noisy view of that one state.
xt+1 = F xt + w  ·  zt = baseline + H(EHR) xt + v
3
5-day forecast · P(fluid ≥ 2 L within 5 days), closed form from the projected mean and variance. Alert at ≥ 60%.
4
Twin fidelity · each day the twin predicts the next readings and scores how often reality lands inside its own 95% band.
5
Active sensing · uncertain + rising + scale skipped → ask the family for a weigh-in.
6
What-if simulation · project fluid for 10 days under a clinician-chosen diuretic change.

4 · People who act

Clinician dashboard (Streamlit)Belief band, risk, fidelity, alerts vs the 2 kg rule, EHR panel, day-by-day replay, what-if panel.
Caregiver messagePlain language, Hindi + English (templates in PoC).
DeliveryWhatsApp / SMS / IVR in Indian languages; nurse call-list for heart-failure clinics; EHR write-back as FHIR Observation.
SafetyDecision support only. The twin never changes treatment; the clinician decides.
Evaluation pipeline (make_figures.py) · dev cohort (seed 7) picks the alert threshold; untouched test cohort (seed 11, 500 patients) reports sensitivity 2-14 days ahead, lead time, false alerts per patient-month vs the 2 kg / 3 day guideline rule; ablations by data stream.
Synthetic cohort realism · person-specific sensor responses, fast and slow decompensators, salty-meal fluid bumps, scale offsets, missing days, and other-cause readmissions the twin should not claim.
Stack · Python, NumPy, pandas, Plotly, Streamlit, Matplotlib, pytest (10 tests incl. no look-ahead leakage). MIT licence. Runs on a laptop in under a minute.
